Based on the latest transactional data for Q3, the total revenue across all regions reached $4.2M, representing a 12% increase compared to Q2.

The North Region was the top performer, contributing 45% of the total revenue, driven largely by strong sales in the flagship New York store. SELECT 
  r.region_name,
  SUM(s.amount) AS total_revenue
FROM sales s
JOIN stores st ON s.store_id = st.id
JOIN regions r ON st.region_id = r.id
WHERE 
  s.transaction_date >= '2023-07-01' 
  AND s.transaction_date <= '2023-09-30'
GROUP BY r.region_name
ORDER BY total_revenue DESC;
